Our Story

About CookList AI

CookList AI was created to solve the daily headache of "what should we cook today?" while keeping food waste low and budgets under control.

By using advanced AI models, it acts as your personal chef and shopping assistant. It instantly checks your pantry, analyzes ingredient costs, suggests smart substitutions, and generates structured recipes tailored to your precise needs.
